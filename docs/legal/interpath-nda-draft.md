# NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is made on **[Date]** between:

1. **[Client Legal Name]**, trading as or representing **Interpath - Ultimate Diagnostic Solutions**, of **[Client Address]** ("Client"); and
2. **[Developer Legal Name]**, of **[Developer Address]** ("Developer").

Together, the Client and Developer are the "Parties".

## 1. Purpose

The Parties intend to discuss and work on the design, development, deployment, maintenance, and possible Android and iOS publication of a laboratory results application, including a PWA, backend services, SLIS API integrations, mobile applications, and related systems ("Project").

## 2. Confidential Information

"Confidential Information" means all non-public information disclosed by either Party, whether written, oral, visual, electronic, technical, commercial, operational, or otherwise, including:

- SLIS API documentation, endpoints, credentials, tokens, logs, and technical integration details;
- patient, doctor, clinic, employee, visit, result, PDF, medical, and laboratory data;
- source code, database schemas, infrastructure details, passwords, environment variables, keys, designs, business processes, pricing, contracts, and deployment information;
- app store records, screenshots, release notes, review correspondence, analytics, and support information;
- any information that a reasonable person would understand to be confidential given the nature of the information or the circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes information disclosed before the date of this Agreement if it relates to the Project.

## 3. Exclusions

Confidential Information does not include information that the receiving Party can prove:

- is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement;
- was already lawfully known before disclosure;
- was independently developed without using Confidential Information;
- was lawfully received from a third party without a duty of confidentiality.

## 4. Confidentiality Obligations

The receiving Party must:

- keep Confidential Information strictly confidential;
- use Confidential Information only for the Project;
- protect Confidential Information using at least reasonable care and no less care than it uses for its own confidential information;
- not disclose Confidential Information to any third party except as allowed by this Agreement;
- limit access to personnel, contractors, legal advisers, accountants, hosting providers, and app store service providers who need access for the Project and are bound by confidentiality obligations.

## 5. Medical and Personal Data

Because the Project may involve medical and personal data, the receiving Party must:

- treat all patient and laboratory information as sensitive confidential information;
- not store medical results permanently on personal devices unless expressly authorised in writing;
- not share raw medical result values through WhatsApp, email, SMS, or other informal channels unless expressly authorised by the Client and legally permitted;
- use secure storage, access control, and reasonable technical safeguards;
- promptly report any suspected unauthorised access, loss, disclosure, or security incident to the disclosing Party.

Each Party must comply with applicable privacy, data protection, health information, and cybersecurity laws relevant to its role and jurisdiction.

## 6. Permitted Disclosure

The receiving Party may disclose Confidential Information if required by law, court order, regulator, app store review process, or other lawful authority, provided that where legally permitted it gives the disclosing Party prompt notice and cooperates to limit the disclosure.

## 7. Ownership

Confidential Information remains the property of the disclosing Party. No licence, assignment, or ownership right is granted except the limited right to use Confidential Information for the Project.

## 8. Return or Destruction

On request, or when the Project ends, the receiving Party must return or destroy Confidential Information, except for:

- archival copies required by law, audit, professional obligations, or automatic backup systems;
- records reasonably required to prove performance, payment, compliance, or dispute resolution.

Any retained copies remain subject to this Agreement.

Confidential Information relating to patient data, security credentials, source code, trade secrets, and medical records remains protected for as long as it remains confidential or legally protected.
